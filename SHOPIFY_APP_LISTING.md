### App Introduction (100 characters max)

*Alternative (98 chars):*

### App Details (500 characters max)
Fitted automatically connects customers who purchase installation-required products with qualified local tradespeople. When a customer adds an eligible product to their cart, they can instantly book installation services during checkout. The app matches customers with nearby installers based on location, handles scheduling, and manages the entire booking process—saving you time and providing a seamless post-purchase experience that increases customer satisfaction and reduces support requests.
