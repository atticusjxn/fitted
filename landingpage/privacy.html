Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 5, 2025

Introduction

Fitted ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a Shopify app that connects customers who have purchased products requiring installation with licensed, local tradespeople. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

From Merchants

When merchants install our app, we collect:

  • Shop domain and basic shop information
  • Product information (for products eligible for installation services)

From Customers (Post-Purchase)

After a customer completes a purchase on a merchant's store, we collect:

  • Name (first and last name)
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address (including postal code)
  • Order details (product purchased, order ID)

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Match customers with licensed tradespeople in their area based on postal code
  • Facilitate communication between customers and tradespeople for installation services
  • Provide installation quotes and schedule service appointments
  • Improve our service quality and matching algorithms

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal data to third parties
  • Use your data for marketing purposes unrelated to installation services
  • Share your information except with the matched tradesperson for installation purposes
  • Make automated decisions with legal or significant effects on you

Data Sharing

We only share your personal information with:

  • Matched Tradespeople: We share your name, contact information, and service requirements with the licensed tradesperson you select to facilitate installation services.
  • Service Providers: We use secure cloud services (Supabase) to store and process data. These providers are bound by strict data protection agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for:

  • Active Leads: Until the installation service is completed or the lead is closed
  • Completed Services: 90 days after service completion for support and quality assurance purposes
  • Cancelled Requests: 30 days after cancellation

After these periods, personal data is automatically deleted from our systems.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encryption at Rest: All data stored in our databases is encrypted
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls limit who can view customer data
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security audits and updates
  • Secure Infrastructure: We use enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with built-in security features

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out: Decline installation services at any time
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

Customer Consent

By proceeding with an installation service request through our platform:

  • You consent to us collecting and processing your personal information as described in this policy
  • You consent to us sharing your information with your selected tradesperson
  • You understand that you can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us

Cookies and Tracking

Our Shopify extension does not use cookies or tracking technologies. We only collect data explicitly provided through the checkout process.

Third-Party Services

Our service operates on:

  • Shopify: Subject to Shopify's Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Our database provider, compliant with GDPR and other privacy regulations

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending email notifications for material changes
  • Displaying prominent notices in our app

Compliance

We are committed to complying with:

  • Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU customers
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California customers
  • Shopify's data protection requirements
