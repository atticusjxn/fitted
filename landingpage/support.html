Everything you need to know about using Fitted

Welcome to Fitted! 👋

Fitted helps you connect customers with qualified installers right at checkout. This guide will help you get started and make the most of the app.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

Step 1: Install the App

Install Fitted from the Shopify App Store. The app will automatically add a checkout extension to your thank you page.

Step 2: Tag Your Products

Add the tag installation-required to any products that need professional installation (ceiling fans, air conditioners, TV mounts, etc.).

Step 3: Configure Settings

In your Fitted dashboard, set your search radius (default: 50km) and how many installers to show customers (default: 3).

Step 4: You're Done!

When customers purchase tagged products, they'll automatically see installer options on their thank you page.

📋 How Fitted Works

  1. Customer Makes Purchase: Customer buys a product tagged with installation-required
  2. Installer Options Shown: On the thank you page, the customer sees 3 nearby installers with ratings, distance, and specialties
  3. Customer Selects Installer: Customer chooses their preferred installer and provides contact details and urgency
  4. Request Logged: The installation request appears in your merchant dashboard with full customer details
  5. Installer Contacts Customer: The selected installer reaches out to schedule the installation
  6. Installation Completed: Installer performs the work and you have a happy customer!

🛠️ Common Tasks

Add Installation Tag

Go to Products → Select product → Add tag → Type installation-required → Save

View Leads

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How are installers matched to customers?

We use a 50km radius search based on the customer's shipping postcode. Installers are sorted by distance, with the closest shown first. The default radius can be adjusted in settings.

How do I add more installers to the network?

Installers can sign up directly at tryfitted.com/installer-signup.html. We vet all installers before approving them to ensure they're qualified and licensed.

Can I disable the extension for certain products?

Yes! Simply remove the installation-required tag from any products you don't want to show installers for. The extension only appears when a customer purchases a tagged product.

What happens if there are no installers in my customer's area?

If no installers are available within the search radius, customers will see a message saying "No installers available in your area yet" and can optionally try a nearby postcode.

Can I see what customers are requesting?

Absolutely! Your merchant dashboard shows all installation requests including customer name, contact details, product, urgency, and status. You can export this data or track completion rates.

What customer data does Fitted collect?

Fitted collects customer name, email, phone number, and shipping postcode (for installer matching only). We never sell or share this data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need to do anything after a customer requests installation?

No - the selected installer will contact the customer directly to schedule installation. However, you can monitor progress in your dashboard and follow up if needed.

Can customers choose their installer?

Yes! Customers see multiple installer options with ratings, specialties, and distance. They can choose the one they feel most comfortable with.

Is there a cost to use Fitted?

Fitted is free to install. There are no platform fees or commissions - installers are paid directly by customers for their services.

What if a customer has a problem with their installer?

While installers operate independently, we maintain quality standards. If there's an issue, contact us at support@tryfitted.com and we'll help resolve it and may remove problematic installers from the network.

🎯 Best Practices

💡 Pro Tip: Tag Strategically

Only tag products that genuinely need professional installation. Over-tagging can create confusion and reduce customer trust.

  • Be Specific: Use product descriptions to mention that installation services are available at checkout
  • Monitor Regularly: Check your dashboard weekly to see installation request trends
  • Communicate with Installers: Build relationships with top-performing installers in your area
  • Follow Up: Consider following up with customers post-installation to ensure satisfaction
  • Adjust Radius: If you're in a rural area, increase the search radius to find more installers

🔧 Troubleshooting

Extension not showing on thank you page

Solution: Ensure the purchased product has the installation-required tag and that your extension is enabled in settings.

No installers appearing

Solution: Try increasing your search radius in settings, or check if there are installers registered in that region. Contact us to help recruit installers in your area.

Customer didn't see installer options

Solution: Verify the product tag is correct (exact spelling: installation-required) and that the customer completed checkout (not abandoned).

Dashboard not loading

Solution: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the problem persists, contact support.

Settings not saving

Solution: Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that you clicked the save button. Contact support if the issue continues.
