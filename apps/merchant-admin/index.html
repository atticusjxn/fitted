How it works:
  1. Add the tag to any product requiring installation
  2. When customers purchase tagged products, the Fitted extension appears on the Thank You page
  3. Customers can book local installers instantly

Support & Documentation

Getting Started

Fitted connects your customers with local, verified installers right after checkout. Here's how to get started:

Step 1: Tag Your Products

In your Shopify admin, add the tag installation-required to any product that needs installation.

Step 2: Configure Settings

Visit the Settings page to customize the installer search radius and notification preferences.

Step 3: Monitor Leads

Track all installation requests in the Leads page. You'll see customer details, selected installer, and booking status.
