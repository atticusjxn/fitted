Getting Started

How Fitted Works

  1. Tag products that require installation with installation-required
  2. When customers purchase tagged products, they'll see installer options on the thank you page
  3. Customers can select an installer and submit their details
  4. Installation requests appear in your dashboard
  5. The selected installer will contact the customer to schedule installation
